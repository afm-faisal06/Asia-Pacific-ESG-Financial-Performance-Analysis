# Asia-Pacific ESG & Financial Performance Analysis

A Tableau Story and business analyst report examining how ESG (Environmental, Social & Governance) performance relates to governance quality and financial outcomes across nine Asia-Pacific markets (Australia, Bangladesh, China, India, Indonesia, South Korea, Malaysia, New Zealand, Singapore), 2010–2024.


## Overview

Built from a firm-year panel (LSEG Workspace / Compustat Global, ~17,000 ESG-disclosing firm-years after filtering), the project combines:

- **A 3-dashboard Tableau Story** — market overview, governance drivers, and ESG-financial outcomes — with global filters, dashboard-to-dashboard actions, a time-series forecast to 2028, and a parameter-driven "ESG High Threshold" what-if scenario.
- **A written business analyst report** interpreting the dashboards against academic literature and Asia-Pacific regulatory context (ASIC/AASB, MAS, SEBI, HKEX/CSRC, KSSB, aligned with ISSB standards).

## Key findings

- Developed markets (Australia, Singapore, New Zealand) consistently outperform emerging Asia-Pacific peers on ESG scores, with a gradual regional upward trend since 2010.
- Governance quality — board gender diversity, separation of CEO/Chair roles, lower controversy exposure — is associated with stronger ESG performance.
- ESG shows a small but positive relationship with financial outcomes (ROA, Tobin's Q); the effect is real but modest, and reporting is biased toward larger firms.
- The forecast to 2028 shows ESG scores trending mildly upward, but widening confidence intervals signal growing uncertainty in longer-range projections.
